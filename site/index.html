Sources and method

  • Popularity combines . Each source is indexed so the launch month is 100, then averaged over the sources available that month.
  • Market value comes from daily closing share prices on Yahoo Finance, shown as the % change since launch day.
  • Its market is the stock market index the company is measured against (for example the Nikkei 225 for Tokyo listings). The dashed line shows how the company’s value would have moved if it had simply tracked that index.
  • The verdict is the gap between the two lines 30 days after launch: how far the company moved beyond what its market did.
  • Hindsight trades show what buying the shares and shorting the index at the launch-day close (or the reverse) would have made over the same period, which is roughly the gap. They ignore trading costs and illustrate the data; they are not investment advice.
